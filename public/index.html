I picked Video Games because when I was really young video games were a core foundation for me growing up. Whether it was running to my friends house after school to play Rockband because he just got the set and wanted to try it. To spending the night and spending hours doing Halo 2 tournaments on split screen mode because we had nothing else to do. When our house got broken into after Christmas one year when I was younger I remember going to my friend's house and spending hours on Minecraft with him to help me escape. Video Games have always been a sense of escape and sometimes relaxation for me. In so, I love video games, and I really want to recreate those foundational moments from my childhood so other people can have the same experiences I had.
